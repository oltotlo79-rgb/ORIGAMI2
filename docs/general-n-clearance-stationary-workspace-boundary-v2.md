# General-N clearance: stationary workspace boundary (V2)

The general-N (`N >= 33`) clearance prerequisite remains explicitly
unpromoted: it is not positive-thickness continuous-motion or collision
clearance authority. This note records both the stationary resource boundary
and the separately typed stationary whole-parent positive-thickness proof now
available inside that boundary. It does not promote the clearance prerequisite
or claim a nonstationary theorem.

## Admitted closure route

Clearance V2 admits the V1 whole-parent and per-block closure replays only
when all of the following hold before pair construction or nested proof
replay:

- every parent-schedule derivative bound is bit-identical to `0.0`;
- both dyadic limits have `max_depth == 0` and `max_leaves == 1`.

An exact block restriction copies the parent edge profiles, so a stationary
parent is stationary on every block. For the canonical Miura N>=33 shape,
the multi-leaf special premises reject by cardinality before allocating; the
V1 issuer then deterministically takes its stationary, one-leaf route.

Nonstationary or adaptive-dyadic input returns `ResourceLimit`. That is a
deliberate fail-closed boundary, not a claim that a retained V1 certificate
also bounds the unimplemented workspace.

## Proven stationary positive-thickness boundary

Commit `be34d05ead8211129126a0a784414caf63ce2f30` adds the separate
`CommonArticulationProfileBoundWholeParentPositiveThicknessOutcomeV2`.
The canonical N=33 fixture reaches `Proven` only after all of the following
are re-established from live inputs:

- the sealed compact layer-pair source and its registry/assignment digests;
- the exact profile, namespace, revision, fold fingerprint, transport limits,
  and transport prerequisite;
- an exact process-local parent-graph admission and all of its retained limits
  and resource counters;
- bit-identical `+0.0` hinge angles and derivative bounds for source, target,
  and pose, plus positive finite paper thickness; and
- strict separating-axis evidence for every non-contact unordered face pair.
  A shared material feature is admitted only by the exact parent admission at
  that exact stationary pose.

Pair and SAT-axis scans poll the cooperative checkpoint. Only
`PairEvidenceUnavailable` becomes an explicit `Unpromoted` outcome; malformed
input, resource failures, cancellation, and deadline expiry remain errors.
Revalidation repeats the live source/profile/transport tuple checks with a
fresh compact authority. The certificate remains sealed, and neither the
certificate nor outcome is serializable or authorizing: continuous motion,
collision clearance, layer transport, project mutation, Apply, and viewer
predicates are all false.

## Charged peak

For each stationary dyadic replay, the clearance storage envelope charges the
maximum of:

- the final one-leaf dyadic certificate; and
- the `evaluate(0)` hinge-angle vector plus
  `checked_solve_closed_peak_bytes_v1`.

The latter already includes the solve's map/vector slack and returned pose.
The block replay adds the largest block dyadic peak to its record, schedule,
closure, coverage-edge, and articulation-face charges; the parent replay adds
the parent dyadic peak to its parent schedule/closure and binding charges.
The maximum of pose, all-block, and parent phases must fit the existing
profiled `clearance_storage_bytes_v2` value.

`max_block_closure_bytes` and `max_parent_closure_bytes` remain retained-proof
limits. They are not redefined as workspace budgets, preserving their exact
retained-byte boundary semantics.

## Required extension before dynamic admission

Opening nonstationary general-N closure requires a workspace-limited V1
variant that accounts for, and bounds, all adaptive proof allocations:

- pending/leaves worklists and their growth behavior;
- interval boxes, graph maps, adjacency, pose queues, and checked hinges;
- exact rational schedule-evaluation temporaries; and
- theorem-recognizer scratch allocations.

Only after that V1 contract exists may V2 raise the stationary/single-leaf
gate.
